    A NOTE FROM THE KITCHEN

    Made for real life.

    Created by Alisia, Recipe Finder began with a simple idea: cook something lovely from what you already have. This edition keeps the original collection and adds a fresh design, smarter search, measured kitchen recipes, and tools for the week ahead.

    Original recipes are adapted from the Wikibooks Cookbook. Recipe content is shared under CC BY-SA 4.0. Each original recipe links to its source. The new kitchen collection is authored for this edition; it is not kitchen-tested.

    Illustrations are artistic representations. Costs are rough estimates in euros, not current shop prices. Diet labels are ingredient-based guides; check products, substitutions, and cross-contact for allergies.

    Your pantry, cookbook, and meal plan are saved only in this browser. No account, tracking, or recipe API is required. Clearing browser data removes your saved kitchen. Export a backup from Kitchen settings to keep it.
