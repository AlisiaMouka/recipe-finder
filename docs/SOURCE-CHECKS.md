# Recipe source checks

Checked against the linked English Wikibooks Cookbook pages on **26 October 2026**. The 22 overrides in `recipe-enhancements.js` preserve the supplied recipe names, identities and source URLs. Together with the 40 newly authored recipes, this gives **62 recipes with ingredient quantities out of 190**. The other 128 retain their original ingredient lists without invented amounts.

“Source-checked” means the ingredient amounts and stated serving basis were compared with the linked page. It does **not** mean that these recipes were cooked, tasted or professionally tested. Displayed costs remain estimates. Preparation and cooling allowances noted below are editorial estimates, not measured kitchen timings. Larger batches can take longer.

Required ingredients have numeric amounts. Salt, pepper and method water are treated as kitchen staples; water needed for the method is stated in the steps. Optional unmeasured garnishes remain optional. Cups and unusual source measures are retained rather than given unsupported gram conversions. Where a source offers alternatives or ranges, the selected version is recorded below. Final cooking text is paraphrased, with ingredient counts referring to the structured ingredient list so serving changes do not leave stale counts in the method.

The source pages carry CC BY-SA 4.0 licensing; links and licence information remain in each recipe. The adaptations in this file are supplied under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Additional attribution on individual source pages is retained below and, where applicable, in `sourceAttribution`.

### 2. Creamed Spinach

[Wikibooks source](https://en.wikibooks.org/wiki/Cookbook:Creamy_Mushroom_Pasta). Basis: **1 serving**. ### 5. Rosemary Garlic Baked Potatoes

### 22. Zacusca

[Wikibooks source](https://en.wikibooks.org/wiki/Cookbook:Zacusca_(Romanian_Vegetable_Spread)). Basis: **15 servings**, 3 hours. Uses 3 kg aubergines, 500 g onions, 200 g tomato paste and 700 ml sunflower oil. The separate 1 kg gogoșar and 1 kg bell pepper entries are combined as 2 kg with both types stated. Four bay leaves selected from 4–6. Salt and pepper become seasoning to taste.

The source's jar-storage wording is **not carried over**. This adaptation is for fresh or refrigerated use, with prompt cooling in shallow containers. The [National Center for Home Food Preservation's general canning FAQ](https://nchfp.uga.edu/faqs/general-canning/category/faq-canning) explains that filling and sealing jars without a tested canning process does not make the contents safe for room-temperature storage. Sterilized jars alone do not establish a preservation process. The recipe therefore states that it is not a tested canning process and must not be sealed for shelf storage.

## Candidates deliberately left without new quantities

No quantities were invented to reach a target count. Reviewed candidates that remain unchanged include French Omelette (unmeasured cooking fat), Basic Risotto (a glass of wine and unclear extra fat), Carrot Salad (unmeasured vinegar and pine nuts), Bangers and Mash (unmeasured butter, milk and oil), Toad in the Hole (a separate batter recipe needed), Creamy Tomato and Bean Soup (ambiguous combined/per-can weight), French Onion Soup (unmeasured spice and bouquet components), and Dovga (variable greens and unclear preparation timing). This is a bounded review, not a complete source audit of all 150 supplied records.

## Newly authored collection consistency fixes

The 40 recipes in `recipes-extra.js` are newly written, not source-verified legacy adaptations. The courgette notes in the frittata and lemon courgette–white bean pan now say **about 250 g each**, so the note stays meaningful when whole-item quantities scale. The mug cake uses one sufficiently large mug per serving, with each mug microwaved separately; scaling does not multiply one mug's cooking time. These are content and software checks, not kitchen tests.
