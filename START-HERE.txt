RECIPE FINDER — A LITTLE PANTRY MAGIC

Hi Alisia! This is the expanded edition of your Recipe Finder.

1. Unzip this whole folder.
2. Open index.html in your browser.
3. Add a few ingredients and find something delicious.

No account, API key, install, or online recipe service needed.
If your browser restricts local files, install Node.js 18+ and run:
    npm start
Then open http://127.0.0.1:4173

The README has editing instructions, credits, and useful limitations.
There are 190 recipes: 40 new recipes and 90 source-checked originals have measured ingredients.
Your pantry, favorites, meal plan and cooking progress stay in your own browser.
Use Kitchen settings to export a backup or explore the optional 3D bowl.
